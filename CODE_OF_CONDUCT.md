# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We are committed to making participation in NIL welcoming, respectful, and
free from harassment for everyone.

## Expected behavior

Be respectful, assume good faith, give constructive feedback, and focus on
the work rather than the person.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, threats, personal attacks, doxxing, sexualized
conduct, and publishing private information without permission are not
acceptable.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository maintainers. Maintainers
may remove comments, reject contributions, or restrict participation when
needed to protect the community.
